AI-powered nudges

The AI checks the current conversation and may show a relevant in-chat next step. It will not send messages later in the background.

Example nudges
Product interest

Help them choose

“Would you like a quick comparison of these options, or should I recommend the best fit?”

Unanswered question

Offer a useful next step

“I can also explain how the setup works for a team like yours.”

Booking intent

Make booking easy

“Would you like me to share the booking link so you can pick a time?”

A nudge is prepared after the visitor message threshold, shown only after the configured idle delay, and suppressed until the cooldown passes. A new message resets the idle timer. Closing the chat uses the same delay before showing the nudge teaser.

Hand over to your team on WhatsApp

When a person is needed, the bot shows a “Continue on WhatsApp” button. It opens a normal WhatsApp chat with your number, with the visitor's details and last question already typed in — they just press send. No WhatsApp API is used.

How leads are captured

Lead questions are always shown as their own message after the bot's answer — never mixed into the answer.

Conditions for asking during the chat

Only unanswered questions are asked, and the bot stops asking once every required question has an answer.

Gated resources (links)

When a visitor asks for one of these (brochure, price list, case study…), the bot asks for the selected details first, then shares the link. Paste a link to anything — a PDF, Google Doc, Drive or Dropbox file, video, or web page. The real link stays hidden until the visitor has shared their details (it's opened through a private link that expires after 7 days).

Conversation starters

Suggested questions visitors can tap on the welcome screen. Leave the list empty to use your first FAQ questions automatically.

Quick action buttons

Shortcut buttons shown on the welcome screen and in the chat. Up to 8 enabled actions are shown.

Send a message: sends the text in “Value”. Recommend for me: personalised picks (runs the quiz first if nothing is known yet). Book a call: opens your Calendly link. Contact support: alerts your team. Open link: opens the URL in “Value”.

Personalised product recommendations

Products are ranked for each visitor from their industry and needs (chat or quiz), pages they browsed on your site, products they opened or clicked, topics they asked about, and purchase intent. The AI uses this shortlist when recommending, and it appears on the welcome screen once there is a signal.
