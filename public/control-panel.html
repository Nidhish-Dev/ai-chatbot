AI-powered nudges

The AI checks the current conversation and may show a relevant in-chat next step. It will not send messages later in the background.

Example nudges
Product interest

Help them choose

“Would you like a quick comparison of these options, or should I recommend the best fit?”

Unanswered question

Offer a useful next step

“I can also explain how the setup works for a team like yours.”

Booking intent

Make booking easy

“Would you like me to share the booking link so you can pick a time?”

A nudge is prepared after the visitor message threshold, shown only after the configured idle delay, and suppressed until the cooldown passes. A new message resets the idle timer. Closing the chat uses the same delay before showing the nudge teaser.
